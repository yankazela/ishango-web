Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)
"CalcGlobal is a global marketplace connecting verified financial experts — tax advisors, mortgage specialists, corporate finance professionals — with individuals and businesses navigating complex cross-border financial calculations. Think Upwork meets TurboTax, but for 50+ countries."

Pitch Deck Structure
1. Problem
Financial regulations differ drastically across countries
Individuals and businesses struggle to find trusted, country-specific financial expertise
No single platform aggregates verified financial professionals by jurisdiction and specialty
2. Solution
A two-sided marketplace: users get matched with verified experts based on country, calculator type (income tax, corporate tax, mortgage, capital gains, inheritance tax, import duties)
Built-in calculators give users instant estimates, then seamlessly connect them with a human expert for nuanced advice
Expert verification process ensures quality and trust
3. Market Size
Global tax services market: ~$250B+ annually
Cross-border advisory: fastest growing segment due to remote work and international business expansion
TAM → SAM → SOM: Start with English-speaking markets (US, UK, AU, CA), expand to EU, Asia, LATAM — your app already supports EN, DE, FR, ES, PT, JA
4. Business Model
Commission per lead/consultation sent to experts (15-25%)
Premium expert listings (verified badge, priority placement)
Subscription tiers for experts (monthly fee for enhanced profiles)
API licensing of calculator engines to fintech partners (you already have API endpoints built)
5. Traction / Proof Points
Platform built and functional (calculators for 5+ tax types across multiple countries)
Expert onboarding pipeline live (the join flow you just built)
Multi-language support (6 languages) — shows global intent from day one
6. Competitive Advantage
Network effect: more experts → better coverage → more users → attracts more experts
Multi-country, multi-calculator taxonomy is hard to replicate
Verification layer builds trust competitors lack
Localization-first architecture (next-intl, country-specific calculator logic)
7. Go-to-Market
Phase 1: Seed expert supply — recruit 50-100 experts in 5 key markets via LinkedIn outreach, tax professional associations
Phase 2: Drive demand — SEO on "income tax calculator [country]" queries (high intent, free traffic)
Phase 3: Paid acquisition + partnerships with accounting firms and fintech platforms
8. The Ask
Specify the amount (e.g., $500K–$1.5M seed)
Use of funds: engineering (scale platform), marketing (expert acquisition + user growth), compliance/legal (multi-jurisdiction)
Key Tips for the Pitch
Do	Don't
Lead with the problem — show a real person's pain	Don't start with the tech stack
Demo the calculator → expert handoff flow live	Don't just show slides
Show your 6-language support as proof of global ambition	Don't claim "no competition" — name competitors and explain your edge
Highlight the two-sided marketplace dynamics	Don't ignore unit economics — know your CAC and projected LTV
Have a clear revenue model with numbers	Don't ask for money without explaining exactly how you'll use it
