## Overview

South Africa uses a **progressive income tax system**, where tax rates increase as your income rises. For the 2025/2026 tax year, rates range from **18% to 45%**.

Unlike some countries, South Africa applies:
- **Fixed tax brackets**
- **Tax rebates** (which directly reduce tax payable)
- **No tax-free bracket in the same way as Europe**, but rebates effectively create one

Your final tax depends on:
- Annual taxable income
- Age (rebates differ)
- Deductions (retirement, medical, etc.)
- Residency status

## Tax Brackets (2025/2026)

| Taxable Income | Rate |
|---|---|
| R0 – R237,100 | 18% |
| R237,101 – R370,500 | 26% |
| R370,501 – R512,800 | 31% |
| R512,801 – R673,000 | 36% |
| R673,001 – R857,900 | 39% |
| R857,901 – R1,817,000 | 41% |
| Above R1,817,001 | 45% |

## Tax Rebates (Key Feature)

Instead of a large tax-free band, South Africa reduces tax using **rebates**:

| Rebate Type | Amount |
|---|---|
| Primary (all taxpayers) | R17,235 |
| Secondary (65+) | R9,444 |
| Tertiary (75+) | R3,145 |

### What This Means
- Everyone gets at least **R17,235 off their tax bill**
- This creates an **effective tax-free threshold**

### Tax-Free Threshold (Approximate)
- Under 65: ~R95,750
- Age 65–74: ~R148,000
- 75+: ~R165,000

## How Income Tax is Calculated

### Step-by-Step:

1. Start with **gross annual income**
2. Subtract:
   - Retirement contributions
   - Allowable deductions
3. Get **taxable income**
4. Apply tax bracket rates
5. Subtract rebates
6. Add medical tax credits (if applicable)

---

## Example Calculation

If you earn **R500,000/year**:

- Falls in the **31% bracket**
- Base tax ≈ **R77,362 + 31% of amount above R370,500**
- Estimated tax before rebate: ~R117,000
- Minus rebate (R17,235)
- Final tax: **~R100,000**

Effective tax rate ≈ **20%**

## Medical Tax Credits

South Africa provides **medical scheme tax credits**:

| Beneficiaries | Monthly Credit |
|---|---|
| First 2 members | R364 each |
| Additional members | R246 each |

### Example:
Family of 3:
- (R364 × 2) + R246 = **R974/month**
- Annual credit: **R11,688**

---

## Retirement Contributions

You can deduct contributions to:
- Pension funds
- Provident funds
- Retirement annuities

### Deduction Limit:
- Up to **27.5% of taxable income**
- Capped at **R350,000/year**

---

## Additional Taxes

### UIF (Unemployment Insurance Fund)
- 1% from employee
- 1% from employer
- Capped salary threshold applies

### Skills Development Levy (Employer only)
- 1% of payroll

---

## Effective vs Marginal Tax Rate

- **Marginal rate**: Highest bracket you fall into (e.g., 31%)
- **Effective rate**: Actual percentage paid (often much lower)

Example:
- Income: R500,000
- Marginal: 31%
- Effective: ~20%

---

## Filing Your Tax Return

Tax returns are submitted to **SARS (South African Revenue Service)**.

### Who Must File?
- Self-employed individuals
- High-income earners
- Those with multiple income sources

### Filing Methods:
- eFiling (online)
- SARS MobiApp
