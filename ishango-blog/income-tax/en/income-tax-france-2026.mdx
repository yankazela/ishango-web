## Overview

France uses a **progressive income tax system** based on **household income**, not just individual earnings.

A key feature is the **quotient familial**, which adjusts your tax based on:
- Marital status
- Number of dependents

Tax rates range from **0% to 45%**, applied after dividing income into “shares” (*parts fiscales*).

Your final tax depends on:
- Total household income
- Number of shares (family size)
- Deductions and tax credits

---

## Tax Brackets (2026)

| Income Range (per share) | Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to €11,294 | 0% |
| €11,295 – €28,797 | 11% |
| €28,798 – €82,341 | 30% |
| €82,342 – €177,106 | 41% |
| Above €177,107 | 45% |

> ⚠️ These brackets apply **per share**, not total household income.

---

## What is the Quotient Familial?

France divides your household income into **shares**:

| Situation | Shares |
|----------|--------|
| Single | 1 |
| Married / Civil union | 2 |
| First 2 children | +0.5 each |
| Third child onward | +1 each |

### Why It Matters

More shares = lower tax per share → lower total tax.

---

## How Income Tax is Calculated

### Step-by-Step:

1. Start with **total household income**
2. Subtract deductions:
   - Professional expenses
   - Pension contributions
3. Divide by number of shares
4. Apply tax brackets to **income per share**
5. Multiply result by number of shares
6. Apply caps and tax credits

---

## Example Calculation

### Scenario:
- Married couple (2 shares)
- Income: €60,000

### Step 1:
€60,000 ÷ 2 = €30,000 per share

### Step 2:
Apply tax brackets:
- €11,294 → 0%
- Remaining €18,706 → mostly taxed at 11% and 30%

Tax per share ≈ €3,000

### Step 3:
Multiply by shares:
- €3,000 × 2 = **€6,000 total tax**

Effective tax rate ≈ **10%**

## Tax Credits and Reductions

France offers many **tax credits** that directly reduce tax:

### Common Credits:
- Childcare expenses
- Home energy improvements
- Donations to charities
- Home services (cleaning, childcare)

---

## Social Contributions

In addition to income tax, France applies **social charges**:

### CSG & CRDS
- ~9.7% on most income types

### Other Contributions
- Pension contributions
- Health insurance (mostly payroll-based)

> These are often deducted at source and are separate from income tax.

---

## Withholding Tax (Prélèvement à la source)

France uses a **pay-as-you-earn system**:

- Tax is deducted monthly from salary
- Based on estimated annual income
- Adjusted after annual declaration

---

## Effective vs Marginal Tax Rate

- **Marginal rate**: Highest bracket reached (e.g., 30%)
- **Effective rate**: Actual average tax paid

Example:
- Household income: €60,000
- Marginal rate: 30%
- Effective rate: ~10%

---

## Filing Your Tax Return

Even with withholding, filing is **mandatory**.

### Timeline:
- Annual declaration (spring)
- Adjustments made later in the year

### Process:
- Pre-filled online declaration
- Review and confirm or modify

---

## Tax Optimization Tips

- Maximize deductions before applying quotient
- Use available tax credits
- Declare all dependents correctly
- Track eligible expenses (childcare, home services)

---

## Common Mistakes

- Forgetting the quotient familial impact
- Not applying tax credits
- Confusing social charges with income tax
- Underestimating final adjustments after withholding
